Spot Fantasy

Privacy Policy · Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Spot Fantasy on iPhone or iPad. Spot Fantasy is made by Dajun Wu (“we”).

Summary

  • No account, no sign-in, and we do not run our own servers or analytics.
  • Your game data stays on your device.
  • Optional reward videos are provided by Google AdMob, only when you choose to watch one.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, stars, best times, settings and your balance of hints in a file on your device only. We cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes this data; purchased hints cannot be restored after the app is deleted, because they are consumable.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional reward videos. A video loads and plays only for the reward screen, and you choose whether to watch. To deliver and measure these ads, Google may collect and process information such as device identifiers (e.g. the identifier for vendors), IP address (used for approximate location), and information about your interactions with ads, plus diagnostic data. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA).

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it at any time in the game (title screen ⓘ button, or the pause menu › Ad privacy choices). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Hint packs are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made, so it can add your hints.

Children

Spot Fantasy is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are restricted to content suitable for all ages.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we have no personal information to share. The only third parties involved are Google (advertising, as described above) and Apple (purchases and app distribution).

Your choices and rights

  • You can play every level without watching ads or buying anything.
  • You can change your ad consent in the game at any time.
  • You can reset the advertising identifiers on your device in iOS Settings.
  • Because we keep no data about you, requests to access or delete data held by Google should be made through Google's privacy tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a request on our support page on GitHub, or see the Spot Fantasy support page.

隐私政策（简体中文）

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日

本政策说明你在 iPhone 或 iPad 上玩《梦幻找不同》（Spot Fantasy）时，你的信息会如何处理。本游戏由 Dajun Wu（“我们”）制作。

概要

  • 无需账号或登录；我们没有自己的服务器，也不使用数据分析。
  • 游戏数据只保存在你的设备上。
  • 可选的奖励视频由 Google AdMob 提供，只在你主动选择观看时播放。
  • 购买由 Apple 处理，我们看不到你的任何付款信息。
  • 我们不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你，游戏也不会请求“App 跟踪透明度”授权。

保存在设备上的数据

游戏只在你的设备上保存进度、星星、最佳时间、设置和提示余额，我们无法看到。删除 App 会同时删除这些数据；已购买的提示属于消耗型项目，删除 App 后无法恢复。

广告（Google AdMob）

游戏提供可选的奖励视频，只在你选择观看时加载和播放。为了投放和统计这些广告，Google 可能会收集和处理设备标识符（例如供应商标识符）、IP 地址（用于大致位置）、广告互动信息以及诊断数据。广告仅限适合所有年龄的内容。我们不使用 Apple 广告标识符（IDFA）。

在欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士以及其他法律要求的地区，Google 的同意表单会在请求任何广告之前征求你的选择，你也可以随时在游戏中更改（标题页的 ⓘ 按钮或暂停菜单 › 广告隐私选项）。详情请见 Google 隐私权政策。

App 内购买

提示包通过 Apple App Store 销售，付款由 Apple 依据 Apple 隐私政策 处理。游戏只会收到购买成功的确认，用来发放提示。

儿童

本游戏面向一般大众，并非专为 13 岁以下儿童设计，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息；广告仅限适合所有年龄的内容。

共享与出售

我们不会出售你的个人信息，也没有可共享的个人信息。涉及的第三方只有 Google（广告）和 Apple（购买与分发）。

你的选择

  • 不看广告、不购买也能玩完所有关卡。
  • 可以随时在游戏中更改广告同意选择。
  • 可以在 iOS 设置中重置设备上的广告标识。

联系我们

如对隐私有疑问，请在 GitHub 支持页面 提交请求，或查看 支持页面。
