Need help?

Open a request on our GitHub support page (choose “New issue”, mention Spot Fantasy, your device and iOS version). We read every request.

Frequently asked questions

How do hints work?

Every hint uses one hint from your balance. Get more by watching a short optional video (+1 hint, up to 5 videos a day) or with a hint pack. Hints are never required to finish a level.

The video button is grey.

A video is only offered when one is ready. Check your internet connection and try again later. The daily limit is 5 videos and resets the next day. The game always works without videos.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Close the game completely and open it again while online: the App Store re-sends unfinished purchases, and the game adds them exactly once. If it still doesn't arrive, contact us and Apple support at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I restore purchases on a new device?

Hint packs are consumable items, so they are used up in the game and are not restored by the App Store. Progress is stored on your device only.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language (10 languages). You can also pick a language inside the game's settings.

Privacy

Read the Spot Fantasy privacy policy. You can change ad consent in the game at title screen ⓘ button, or the pause menu.

技术支持（简体中文）

200 个童话场景里的梦幻找不同之旅。

需要帮助？请在 GitHub 支持页面 点“New issue”提交问题，写明《梦幻找不同》、设备型号和 iOS 版本。

提示怎么获得？

每次提示消耗 1 次提示余额。可以自愿观看短视频获得（每次 +1，每天最多 5 次），也可以购买提示包。不用提示也能通关所有关卡。

视频按钮是灰色的？

只有视频准备好时才能观看。请检查网络后稍后再试；每天最多 5 次，第二天重置。不看视频也完全不影响游戏。

买了没到账？

联网状态下完全关闭游戏再打开，App Store 会重新发送未完成的购买，游戏只会发放一次。仍未到账请联系我们，并可到 reportaproblem.apple.com 联系 Apple。

换手机能恢复购买吗？

提示包是消耗型项目，App Store 不会恢复；游戏进度只保存在本机。

怎么切换语言？

游戏默认跟随系统语言（支持 10 种），也可以在游戏设置里手动切换。
