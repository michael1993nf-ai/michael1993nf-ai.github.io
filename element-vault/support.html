Email davidstudiogames@gmail.com (mention Element Vault, your device and iOS version), or open a request on our GitHub support page. We read every message.

Frequently asked questions

What are diamonds for?

Diamonds are used for hints (1 diamond each; hints in tutorial levels are free) and to fill missing elements when you level up a spirit, with a bulk discount. You get diamonds from your first win against each boss, by watching a short optional video (+1, up to 5 a day) or with a diamond pack. Every level can be finished without diamonds.

The video button is grey.

A video is only offered when one is ready. Check your internet connection and try again later. The daily limit is 5 videos and resets the next day. The game always works without videos.

I bought something but didn't receive it.

Close the game completely and open it again while online: the App Store re-sends unfinished purchases, and the game adds them exactly once. If it still doesn't arrive, contact us and Apple support at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I restore purchases on a new device?

Diamond packs are consumable items, so they are used up in the game and are not restored by the App Store. Progress is stored on your device only.

How do I change the language?

The game follows your device language (10 languages). You can also pick a language inside the game's settings.

技术支持（简体中文）

400 关的滑块解谜，还有精灵养成和守护 Boss。

需要帮助？请发邮件至 davidstudiogames@gmail.com（写明《元素秘库》、设备型号和 iOS 版本），或在 GitHub 支持页面 提交问题。

钻石有什么用？

钻石用于提示（每次 1 颗，教学关免费），以及精灵升级时补齐缺少的元素（数量越多越便宜）。首次击败每个 Boss、自愿观看短视频（每次 +1，每天最多 5 次）或购买钻石包都能获得钻石。不用钻石也能通关所有关卡。

视频按钮是灰色的？

只有视频准备好时才能观看。请检查网络后稍后再试；每天最多 5 次，第二天重置。不看视频也完全不影响游戏。

买了没到账？

联网状态下完全关闭游戏再打开，App Store 会重新发送未完成的购买，游戏只会发放一次。仍未到账请联系我们，并可到 reportaproblem.apple.com 联系 Apple。

换手机能恢复购买吗？

钻石包是消耗型项目，App Store 不会恢复；游戏进度只保存在本机。

怎么切换语言？

游戏默认跟随系统语言（支持 10 种），也可以在游戏设置里手动切换。
